1. Who operates LUMIFORGE Automation

LUMIFORGE Automation is an owner-operated, internal automation system for the LumiForge YouTube channel (@LumiForge_X). It is operated by the channel owner for the channel owner's own use. It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts, and no one other than the channel owner authorizes it or signs in to it. It has two components: LUMIFORGE Publisher (runs on the owner's own computer) and Movikit (runs on the owner's private server).

2. YouTube API Services and Google permissions used

LUMIFORGE Automation uses the YouTube API Services. It is authorized through Google OAuth only by the channel owner, for the channel owner's own YouTube account. The permissions (OAuth scopes) it requests are:

ComponentPermission (scope)Used for
LUMIFORGE Publisherhttps://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload Uploading the channel's own videos and setting their custom thumbnails.
LUMIFORGE Publisherhttps://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly Reading the channel's own channel information, its own videos' metadata, status and statistics, and its own playlists.
LUMIFORGE Publisherhttps://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube Adding the channel's own videos to the channel's own playlists and reading playlist contents.
Movikithttps://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl Managing the channel's own live streams (creating, binding, starting and ending live broadcasts), updating the title, description, privacy setting and thumbnail of the channel's own live-broadcast videos, and reading the channel's own name and live-stream status.

3. What YouTube data is accessed, and why

  • The owner's own channel data — channel name and ID, the channel's own videos (titles, descriptions, status, privacy, publish time, thumbnails and public statistics such as views, likes and comments), the channel's own playlists, and the channel's own live streams and broadcasts. This is used only to publish, organize and manage the owner's own content and to keep the owner's production records.
  • Publicly available YouTube data (Movikit) — public search results and public channel and video information and statistics (for example titles, subscriber, view and video counts). This is used only for the owner's own content research. It does not include any private data of other users.

LUMIFORGE Automation does not access other people's private YouTube data, and it does not collect personal information from viewers or from anyone other than the channel owner.

4. Where data and OAuth tokens are stored

  • OAuth tokens for LUMIFORGE Publisher are stored only on the channel owner's own computer.
  • OAuth tokens for Movikit are stored only on the channel owner's private server, with access restricted to the owner.
  • Tokens are never published, shared, or stored in this website or its public repository.
  • Records of the owner's own published videos (such as video links, the metadata used and publication times) are kept in the owner's own production files. Movikit keeps snapshots of public channel statistics used for research for a limited period (currently up to 120 days).

5. Sharing

Google user data obtained through Google APIs is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not transferred to third parties. It is used only by the channel owner for the purposes described above.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

LUMIFORGE Automation's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. YouTube and Google policies

Because LUMIFORGE Automation uses the YouTube API Services, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy also apply.

After revocation, the stored tokens no longer work. To have stored tokens or records deleted, contact the address below.
