LUMIFORGE Automation

Owner-operated internal automation for the LumiForge YouTube channel

Privacy Policy Terms of Service LumiForge on YouTube

LUMIFORGE Automation is a private, internal tool set operated by the owner of the LumiForge YouTube channel. It helps the channel owner publish and manage the channel's own videos and live streams. It is not offered to the public: there are no user accounts, and nobody other than the channel owner signs in to it.

What it consists of

  • LUMIFORGE Publisher — runs on the owner's own computer. Uploads the channel's own finished videos to YouTube, sets their thumbnails, adds them to the channel's own playlists, and reads the channel's own video, playlist and statistics data to keep production records.
  • Movikit — runs on the owner's private server. Manages the channel's own 24/7 live streams (creating and running live broadcasts and updating their title, description, privacy and thumbnail) and reads publicly available YouTube data for the owner's own content research.

Both components use the YouTube API Services with Google OAuth, authorized only by the channel owner for the channel owner's own YouTube account. See the Privacy Policy for exactly which permissions are used and how Google user data is handled.
